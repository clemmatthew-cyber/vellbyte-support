The Advantage privacy

How The Advantage for iPhone and Android handles information.

Information in the app

The Advantage stores your game on your phone: your practice bankroll, the cards left in the shoe, your play statistics, lessons, goals, Daily Shoe results and settings such as sound and table style. The app keeps a second copy of this in its own storage so an update does not lose your progress.

The app has no account, no sign-in and no synchronization. It does not send information to Vellbyte or anyone else, and it has no advertising, analytics or tracking. It plays offline.

Practice chips

Chips in the app are practice chips. They have no cash value, cannot be bought in the app, and cannot be cashed out, transferred or exchanged for prizes.

Sharing and backups

If you choose to share a Daily Shoe card, the app opens X's compose window in the X app or your browser with a short, spoiler-free summary filled in. Nothing is posted unless you post it, and X's own privacy policy applies to what you post. Your device's backup settings may also make a copy of the app's information.

Removing information

Reset bankroll, under Stats on the Play tab, returns the bankroll to $10,000. Deleting the app removes its information from your phone; copies in a device backup may remain and must be managed separately.

Children

The Advantage is not directed to children. It is for adults: it is rated 18+ on the App Store; Google Play's age badge varies by region, and the app is listed there for ages 18 and over, because it simulates gambling, without real money.
