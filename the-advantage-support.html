Common questions

Are the chips real money?

No. The Advantage is free and plays with practice chips. They have no cash value, cannot be bought in the app, and can never be cashed out, transferred or exchanged for prizes.

What if I run out of chips?

The table offers a free refill of chips, or a free rebuy back to $10,000, so you can always keep playing.

Where is my progress?

On your phone. The app has no account and does not sync between devices. It keeps a second copy in its own storage so an update does not lose your progress. Your device backup may also include a copy.

Does it need the internet?

No. The whole game is inside the app and plays offline. Sharing a Daily Shoe card opens X, and Privacy policy and Help and support open in your browser. Those need a connection.

How do I start over?

On the Play tab, open Stats below the table, tap Reset bankroll, then Confirm reset, to go back to $10,000. To remove everything, including lessons and stats, delete the app.

Where are the open-source notices?

Open-source notices: in the app, Me, then Credits.

Which rules does the table use?

Six decks (or eight, if you pick it), dealer stands on soft 17, double after split, late surrender, and blackjack pays 3 to 2. Tap the "6 decks · 3:2" label on the table, or Table rules under Settings on the Me tab, to see them.
