Common questions

Where is my information?

The current app stores its information on the device and does not provide an app account or automatic synchronization. Your device's backup settings may make another copy.

How do I keep a copy?

Use the app's export or backup option and choose where to save the file. Keep the file somewhere you can find it again.

What happens when I import?

Review the app's prompts before confirming an import. Depending on the choice offered, imported information may replace information on this device.

What happens if I remove the app?

Removing an app can remove its locally stored information. Copies you exported, shared or included in a device backup may remain elsewhere.
