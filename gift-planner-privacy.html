Vellbyte Gift Planner privacy

A draft policy for review against the final app.

Information in the app

Vellbyte Gift Planner stores the people, occasions, gift ideas, links, prices and notes you enter on your device. The reviewed app does not provide an account or automatic synchronization service.

Sharing and backups

If you choose to export or share information, the app or service you select and the people you share it with may receive its contents. Your device's backup settings may also make a copy.

Backup and recovery exports can leave files in the app's cache on this device. If you copy information to the clipboard, other apps with clipboard access may be able to read it. If you choose to open a link, your device may pass it to another app.

Removing information

You can remove information from the current list. Earlier saves or recovery copies may remain on the device. Removing the app may remove its local data; exported, shared or device-backup copies may remain elsewhere and must be managed separately.
