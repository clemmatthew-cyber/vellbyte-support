Pantry Turn privacy

How this app handles information.

Information in the app

Pantry Turn stores the food items, quantities, dates, locations and notes you enter on your device. The reviewed app does not provide an account or automatic synchronization service.

Sharing and backups

If you choose to export or share information, the app or service you select and the people you share it with may receive its contents. Your device's backup settings may also make a copy.

Export can leave a backup file in the app's cache on this device.

Removing information

You can remove information in the app. Removing the app may remove its local data; exported, shared or device-backup copies may remain elsewhere and must be managed separately.
